About the Panchayat

Understanding Pallippuram

Pallippuram Grama Panchayat is situated in Ernakulam district, Kerala. Its geographical setting, settlements and local resources are important for understanding the area and supporting local-level planning.

This WebGIS platform provides a digital interface for exploring geographic information and viewing maps of the study area.
